How it works

choose what your
story needs.

A tool gives the AI questions and rules to work through. Here is an example: someone apologizes, and the AI requests a tool to help decide how the other person responds.

Illustrative previews of the preset's workflow, not SillyTavern screenshots.

01 / You choose
Available tool groups
RelationshipsON
EmotionsON
WeatherOFF
Core tools stay loaded.

Enable the groups you want the AI to draw from. Leave the lower tool definitions enabled.

02 / The AI asks
At the end of a reply
“I'm sorry,” he said. She kept her hand on the door.
The AI ends its reply by naming a tool from an enabled group. Here it asks for help with the apology.


03 / The preset loads
In the next reply
Scripted thought summary, then a populated call.
After the tool loads, the AI can write a finding in this format. Forgiveness and restored trust are separate possibilities.


The AI still needs to make a valid request and follow the loaded instructions. You can read the complete tools below.
